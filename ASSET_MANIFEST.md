## 2. Integrity & Preservation Notes
- **Authenticity**: All illustrations of Chu Cuội are preserved unaltered from the original slide presentation (`Cuội's Story.pdf`). No synthetic replacements or character redesigns have been introduced.
- **Aspect Ratio & Display**: All artwork uses `object-fit: contain` for character cutouts/dialogues and `object-fit: cover` for panoramic backgrounds to ensure zero distortion or stretching across all display sizes.
- **Separation of Fact & Fiction**: Historical NASA mission photos and authentic data are distinctly classified with specialized badges and NASA direct links, while Cuội's conversational storytelling is marked as educational folklore narrative.
